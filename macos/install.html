MacOS

Step 1

Install the Prolific Serial driver and extension.

  • Open the App Store and search for PL2303. You will find the PL2303 Serial extension and driver. The app looks like this:
  • Click Get to download the app. Once the extension is installed, click Open.

Note: There may be other versions of the driver that show up in the app store. Be sure to get the correct PL2303 version.

Step 2

Navigate to the Prolific Serial settings.

  • The first time you open the PL2303 Serial app, you may see a dialog box like the one below. If you see the dialog box, click "Open System Settings" and proceed directly to step 3. If you do not see the dialog box, follow the steps below to manually navigate to the settings.
  • Open the Apple menu in the top left corner of your screen and choose System Settings.
  • Open the General tab, shown above, and click "Login Items & Extensions" to open the Extensions pane.
  • In the Login Items & Extensions pane, check for the "com.prolific" reference. If you're using an older version of MacOS, click the "i" in a circle on the right to open the extension tab for the Prolific driver. In newer versions of MacOS just scroll down to the Extensions section of the list to find the PL2303Serial driver.
Step 3

Enable the Prolific Serial extension.

  • In the Extension pane for the Prolific driver, enable the extension as shown. Then click Done. The Prolific Serial driver is now ready to use.
